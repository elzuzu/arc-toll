ArcToll

A guard that refuses a swap through a pool that would take too much — before it executes.

A pool's fee can move. fee() is immutable on a Uniswap V3 pool and every tool assumes so. A live Arc pool presenting that exact interface answered 400 at block 21288698 and 450 at 21308698, on the same day.

That is why this is a guard and not a list. A published figure can be raised before the swap it was meant to protect, so the check is only worth anything in the same transaction as the trade. Every number below is read from the deployed contract right now, in your browser.

A dynamic toll is not a low one. Uniswap V4 marks such pools with 0x800000; masking that flag off leaves zero, so a naive guard calls the most dangerous pool on the chain free. This one refuses to quote a figure at all.
