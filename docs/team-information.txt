directly" against 2.8/3 for a guard, because a list only helps someone who remembers to read it.

caught: a dynamic fee is an unknown one, not a low one, and masking Uniswap V4's 0x800000 flag off
reports the most dangerous pool on the chain as free; and a live Arc pool exposing the supposedly
immutable fee() interface answered 400, then 450, then 460 within a day, which makes any off-chain
check unsound. A fork test pins both blocks and asserts the movement.
