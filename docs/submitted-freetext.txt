================================================================================
FIELD: "In two sentences, what does your project do?"
================================================================================
ArcToll is a guard that a router calls inside its own transaction: it reads a pool's fee on-chain and reverts before a swap through a pool that would take too much can execute. It also prices that loss in native 18-decimal USDC, a dollar figure with no oracle in it, and refuses to quote a pool whose fee a hook sets at swap time.
